The Halo play.

Same positions. New routes. Halo sits beside the founder.

  1. 01Embed.

    A senior revenue leader joins the founder and team.

  2. 02Prove the play.

    Find the right customers, build the pipeline, close the first repeatable deals.

  3. 03Install the system.

    Playbook, targets, coaching, AI workflows that cut manual work.

  4. 04Recruit to proven requirements.

    Hires join a working system, not a blank page.

  5. 05Hand off.

    The team owns the engine.

What this lets you do

  1. 01

    Delay a ~$334K executive seat until the play is proven.

  2. 02

    Hire into a system, not into a guess.

  3. 03

    Keep the team you have. We strengthen it. We don’t replace it.
