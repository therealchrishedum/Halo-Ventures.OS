02 · Why it happensNext ↓

The hire comesbefore the answer.

At first the founder sells everything. Then the founder runs out of time, and growth stalls.

The usual fix is a full-time VP of Sales, hired before anyone knows what sells. When the guess is wrong, the company pays in cash and months, then starts over.
