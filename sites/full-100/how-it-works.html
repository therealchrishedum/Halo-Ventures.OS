How We Work
How We Work

We work in your company. Then your team runs the system.

We are not consultants. We are not an agency. We join your team and do the work with you.

Palantir sent engineers to work inside its customers. They fixed problems on site and turned what worked into products. We do the same for sales.

01 · The five steps
  1. 01

    We join your team.

    What we do
    A senior sales leader joins you. We learn your product, customers and numbers.
    What you get
    A clear view of where sales are stuck.
    What your team keeps
    Your team keeps its work. We work next to them.
  2. 02

    We find what sells.

    What we do
    We find your best customers. We make your message clear. We lead sales.
    What you get
    New sales opportunities and first sales that can repeat.
    What your team keeps
    The customer profile and the message.
  3. 03

    We set up the sales system.

    What we do
    We write the sales playbook. We set targets. We coach the team. We add AI tools for routine work.
    What you get
    A sales system that does not depend on the founder.
    What your team keeps
    The playbook, the targets and the weekly meetings.
  4. 04

    We hire the right people.

    What we do
    We define what success looks like. We make scorecards. We find and hire people.
    What you get
    New hires who join a system that works.
    What your team keeps
    The scorecards and the hiring process.
  5. 05

    Your team runs it.

    What we do
    We step back.
    What you get
    A sales system that lasts.
    What your team keeps
    All of it.

  • Fractional Head of Sales

    Leads the sales team every day.

  • Coaching & Development

    Makes your sales people better.

  • Talent Scouting & Recruiting

    Finds the people you need.

03 · How AI helps

AI does the routine work. People sell.

  • Sales reps spend 70% of their time on work that is not selling.Source: Salesforce
  • We set up AI tools that do this work. Your team gets more time with customers.
04 · Questions
Will you replace my team?

No. We work next to your team and make it stronger. Your team runs the system after we step back.

Do I still need a VP of Sales?

Maybe, later. First we find what sells. Then, if you hire, the new person joins a system that works.

Who is a good fit?

A mission-driven startup with a good product, first customers and a founder who still does most sales.

How many companies do you work with?

About 10 at a time.

How do we start?

Copy the email. Send us two lines about your goal. We meet on a short call to see if we are a good fit. If we are, we agree on a timeline.

Which industries?

Mostly tech, home services and finance.
